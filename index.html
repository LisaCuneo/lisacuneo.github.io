APPLIED MATHEMATICS · INVERSE PROBLEMS · COMPUTATIONAL IMAGING

Turning physical measurements into biological insight

I develop mathematical and computational methods that turn indirect, noisy and incomplete measurements into quantitative information for biology and medicine — through inverse problems, optimization, numerical analysis and machine learning.

My current research focuses on computational microscopy, where physical modelling, acquisition and computation increasingly become part of the same measurement process.
